POKE AUTO CHESS ME v1.1.8 - RESULT SCREENS
==========================================

AFTER EVERY BATTLE
- Full-screen ALLY vs ENEMY comparison.
- Up to nine paired rows: ally portrait, ally damage number/bar,
  enemy damage number/bar, enemy portrait.
- A shared maximum is used for both colors so the bars compare honestly.
- Team damage totals appear in the header.

AFTER ROUND 20 / RUN END
- Shows reached round, remaining HP, gold, reward and best round.
- Shows the current final board in formation order, wrapping for narrow screens.
- Shows every active 2/4/6 synergy with its icon and current Pokemon count.
