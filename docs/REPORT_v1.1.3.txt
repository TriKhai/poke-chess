POKE AUTO CHESS ME v1.1.3
=========================

- Added a live team total below the 3x3 combat statistics grid.
- Metrics are now DMG, NHẬN, CHẶN, HỒI, HP and MP.
- DMG: damage dealt after defense, including shield absorption.
- NHẬN: actual HP damage received after shields.
- CHẶN: damage absorbed by shields.
- HỒI: effective healing, excluding overheal.
- HP: current health. MP: current ability energy.
- Removed type icons from shop cards so portraits are unobstructed.
- build.xml/JAD/manifest version: 1.1.3.
