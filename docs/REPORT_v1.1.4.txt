POKE AUTO CHESS ME v1.1.4
=========================

- Six combat metrics: DMG, KHIÊN, CHẶN, HỒI, HP and MP.
- KHIÊN is total shield created; CHẶN is damage absorbed by shields.
- UP from the top roster row focuses the metric tabs.
- LEFT/RIGHT cycles through all six metrics; DOWN returns to the 3x3 roster.
- UP while the metric row is focused changes ally/enemy side.
- Existing 1/3 metric, 7 side, FIRE detail, * speed and 9 skip keys remain.
- HP bars use hp/maxHp and MP bars use mana/maxMana for every Pokemon.
- build.xml/JAD/manifest version: 1.1.4.
