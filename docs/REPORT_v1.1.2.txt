POKE AUTO CHESS ME v1.1.2 - PERSISTENT COMBAT STATISTICS
=======================================================

VISIBLE CONTROLS
- The combat panel is always visible below the board.
- Top row: TA / ĐỊCH side buttons; selected side has a green/red underline.
- Second row: six metric buttons; selected metric has yellow fill and outline.
- Grid: three columns x three rows; selected Pokemon has a yellow outline.
- Footer always states the active controls.

METRICS
- DMG: damage dealt after defense, including the portion absorbed by shields.
- NHẬN: HP damage actually received after shield absorption.
- CHẶN: incoming damage absorbed by shields.
- HỒI: effective healing, excluding overheal.
- HP: current HP.
- KHIÊN: current virtual HP/shield.

CONTROLS
- 1/3 previous/next metric; 7 switches side; arrows select a Pokemon.
- FIRE opens live details; FIRE/0 returns; * cycles speed; 9 skips battle.

IMPLEMENTATION
- Unit owns fixed integer counters: damageDealt, damageTaken, damageBlocked,
  healingDone and shieldDone.
- Battle updates counters at the authoritative damage/heal/shield paths.
- Mini portraits are generated once at 20px and cached for the active species.
- Version metadata is 1.1.2.
