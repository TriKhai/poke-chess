POKE AUTO CHESS ME v1.1.6b
==========================

FONT
- Normal and bold UI text now use tahoma_7b, matching the visual weight of compact menus.
- Corrected line height remains 11px, read from the tallest bitmap glyph.

ITEM ART
- Uses the owner's item_tps_pixel PNG files directly; no offline redraw or quantization.
- The 90px source is nearest-neighbor scaled once at load time and cached at 24px.
- Collection data continues to follow app.zip ItemComponents and ItemStats.

BUILD

- Numeric build.xml/JAD/manifest version remains 1.1.6.
